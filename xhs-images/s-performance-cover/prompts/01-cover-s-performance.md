# P1 封面图 Prompt

## 策略
- 策略类型：视觉优先型（Strategy C）
- 风格：bold（高冲击力）
- 布局：sparse（极简，1-2个核心信息点）

## 核心信息
- 主标题：双非本 → S绩效
- 副标题：AI加持
- 视觉冲击：10/10

## Bold 风格要素
- 配色：高饱和度（鲜艳红/橙/黄 + 深黑/炭灰 + 白/霓虹黄）
- 背景：渐变（gradient-radial，从中心向外）
- 装饰：几何图形 + 光线效果
- 强调：粗体轮廓 + 3D阴影
- 排版：冲击力字体，大标题

## 详细 Prompt

Create a Xiaohongshu cover image in bold, high-impact style:

**Main Title**: "双非本 → S绩效" (super large, bold font with 3D shadow effect, black text)
**Subtitle**: "AI加持" (medium size, white text on vibrant accent)

**Layout**: Sparse - minimal information, maximum visual impact
- Title dominates the center
- Large arrow (→) connecting the two states
- Minimal decorative elements

**Color Palette**:
- Background: Radial gradient from warm orange-yellow (#F6E05E, #DD6B20) at center to tech blue (#3B82F6) at edges
- Text: Black (#000000) for main title, white (#FFFFFF) for accents
- Highlights: Neon yellow (#F7FF00) for emphasis

**Visual Elements**:
- Geometric shapes (triangles, circles) scattered around
- Light rays emanating from center
- Star burst effect near the arrow
- Bold outline around key text
- High saturation, high contrast

**Typography**:
- Main title: Bold, impactful, 3D shadow effect
- Arrow: Thick, prominent, with glow effect
- Subtitle: Ribbon-style tag with solid background

**Composition**:
- Center-focused, symmetrical
- Dramatic, attention-grabbing
- Clean, modern, professional

**Style**: Bold, high-impact, suitable for Xiaohongshu platform, cartoon illustration style, flat design with depth through shadows and gradients.

**Aspect Ratio**: 3:4 portrait (1024x1024 for square, will be cropped to portrait)
